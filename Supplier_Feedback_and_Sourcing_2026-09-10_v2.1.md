## BOXMORE vs genuine HELIX — corrected interpretation

HifiMotive publicly confirms a close development relationship with Audiotec Fischer and states that HifiMotive-developed loudspeakers have also been sold worldwide as HELIX Compose since 2023. That supports a shared platform/engineering relationship.

It does **not** justify a blanket engineering rule that every current `BXE = Ci3`, `BXA = Ci5`, `BXS = Ci7` part is literally identical except for the logo. Current published specifications and accessories can differ, so every exact SKU must be compared on impedance, power, dimensions, terminals, adapters and Tesla fitment.

### Practical purchasing rule

- **i3 / i5:** prefer genuine HELIX from Germany when pricing is equal or close, especially where the Highland-specific HELIX package/adapters are already verified.
- **i7 class:** BOXMORE BXS deserves selective consideration because the local RRP saving can be material. Only substitute after exact fit/spec/terminal verification.
- **Do not mix by family name alone.** Treat BXE/BXA/BXS as related comparison families, not drop-in equivalence proof.

## v1.9 — Pioneer amplifier replacement options

Schut's suggestion to replace the original Pioneer amplifier with a **MATCH UP 1FX** has now been independently checked against current Audiotec Fischer specifications.

A new load-compatibility issue is more important than the brand choice: Pioneer Europe/retail documentation lists the complete TS-WX1220AH at approximately **0.3 Ω**, while another official Pioneer regional specification lists **0.6 Ω × 2**. Those figures are consistent with two very-low-impedance single-voice-coil drivers originally used in parallel. The loose donor drivers must still be physically verified before rewiring.

|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Retain original Pioneer amp | €0 existing | 10.8–15.1 V | Original 600 W RMS / original matched load | Cheapest, but 15.5 V Tesla supply gate remains; SSR solves the proven wake/inrush issue, not the voltage ceiling. |
| Pioneer + high-current DC-DC | TBD | Regulated to Pioneer range | Original amp/driver match retained | Plausible but converter/current/thermal design and price remain TBD. |
| **MATCH UP 1FX** | **€299** | **10.5–18 V** | 350 W @4 Ω, 600 W @2 Ω, 900 W @1 Ω; 1 Ω stable | **Preferred replacement candidate**, conditional on driver verification and series wiring. |
| HELIX M ONE | €299 | 10.5–16 V | 240/410/600 W @4/2/1 Ω; 1 Ω stable | Full-HELIX branding and original-like 600 W RMS at 1 Ω, but less supply-voltage headroom than UP 1FX. |
| HELIX M ONE X | €379 | 10.5–16 V | 400/700/1030 W @4/2/1 Ω; 1 Ω stable | High-output full-HELIX option; likely more power than required and requires conservative gain/output limiting. |

### Critical wiring rule

None of the replacement amplifiers above may be connected to the original approximately 0.3 Ω parallel load. If the two loose Pioneer drivers are confirmed to be approximately 0.6 Ω each, the likely replacement topology is **series wiring**, yielding approximately **1.2 Ω nominal**. This must be verified before final connection because the replacement amplifiers are specified down to 1 Ω, not 0.3 Ω.

The HELIX V EIGHTEEN provides two processed RCA outputs at up to 3 V RMS and two REM OUT outputs, making a downstream mono amplifier integration straightforward from the signal-control side. The final high-current power/wake architecture still requires engineering: a replacement amp's 15.5 V tolerance does not by itself prove that Tesla's monitored power system will accept its inrush behavior.
