# DSP tuning research — Audison vs HELIX

Checked 2026-09-10.

## Audison
- bit Drive 3.1 / ACCORDO: https://audison.com/bit-drive-3-1/
- bit Drive manual: https://audison.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/35741332904465-bit-Drive-User-manual
- Manufacturer documentation supports automatic output EQ, delay/time alignment and level tuning in ACCORDO, plus automated system-level calibration.

## HELIX
- DSP PC-Tool 6: https://www.audiotec-fischer.de/en/tools/dsp-pc-tool/download-6
- TuneEQ knowledge base: https://www.audiotec-fischer.de/en/knowledge-base/dsp-pc-tool/tuneeq/
- DSP PC-Tool 6 contains meaningful assisted/automatic tuning functions, including TuneToTarget and Automatic Time Measurement. Audiotec Fischer does not position the workflow as a completely automatic end-to-end tuning system; correct crossover, polarity, timing preparation and expert final calibration remain important.

## Correct project conclusion
The statement “HELIX only measures and does nothing automatically” is too absolute. Audison nevertheless has the stronger integrated automatic-tuning workflow for this project. MB Car Audio's practical estimate of about 3 hours Audison versus 8 hours HELIX is retained as **installer field experience**, not a manufacturer benchmark.
