# EU price research snapshot — 2026-09-10

Scope: Netherlands, Germany, Belgium and France. Prices are VAT-inclusive when the source presented them that way. Shipping, payment fees and final checkout availability are excluded unless explicitly included by the source.

## Method

- Cheapest price = lowest captured current/indexed price among NL/DE/BE/FR.
- Average = average of the lowest captured price for each market with a sufficiently reliable observation.
- A blank country does not mean the product is unavailable; it means no sufficiently reliable current local offer was captured in this research pass.
- Marketplace or comparison-engine lows are marked medium confidence and must be rechecked at checkout.
