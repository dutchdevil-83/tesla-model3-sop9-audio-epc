# Audison MB proposal — documentation index

Checked 2026-09-10.

## AF M8.14 bit
- Official product/specification: https://audison.com/product/af-m8-14-bit/
- AF bit owner manual/support: https://audison.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/35030050183057-AF-bit-User-manual
- Published architecture: 8 amplified channels, 14 DSP channels, 8 x 90 W RMS @ 4 ohm / 8 x 140 W @ 2 ohm, 6 pre-outs.

## AF M4D
- Official product/specification: https://audison.com/product/af-m4d/
- Support: https://audison.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/sections/12868024474129-AF-M4D
- Published output: 4 x 160 W @ 4 ohm; 4 x 260 W @ 2 ohm; 2 x 520 W bridged @ 4 ohm.
- MB role: rear of front seats, including rear woofers and rear midranges; optional rear tweeters if used.

## AF M1D
- Official product: https://audison.com/product/af-m1d/
- Support section: https://audison.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/sections/16018035072145-AF-M1D
- MB project role: donor-sub amplifier candidate. **Do not finalize wiring until both loose Pioneer donor drivers are measured and a safe amplifier load is confirmed.**

## AF LINK
- Audison accessories: https://audison.com/collections/car-accessories/
- Function: links DSP OUT of AF bit amplifier to PRE IN of AF D companion amplifier.

## DRC AC
- Official: https://audison.com/fr/product/drc-ac/
- Controls include master volume, subwoofer level, source selection and DSP preset recall; programmable through bit Drive.

## bit Drive / ACCORDO
- bit Drive 3.1: https://audison.com/bit-drive-3-1/
- bit Drive user manual: https://audison.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/35741332904465-bit-Drive-User-manual
- ACCORDO automatic tuning covers output EQ, delays/time alignment and levels. Audison also documents automatic system-level calibration.
