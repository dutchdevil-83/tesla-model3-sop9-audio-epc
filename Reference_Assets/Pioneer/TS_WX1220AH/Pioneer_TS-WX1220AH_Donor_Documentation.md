## Donor-use engineering gate
The project reuses the two physical woofer drivers, not the original enclosure. Complete-system impedance/specification references are **not sufficient to guarantee the electrical characteristics of each loose driver**. Before connecting the Audison AF M1D:
1. identify/measure each donor driver's DC resistance and nominal impedance class;
2. document coil configuration and original internal wiring;
3. select a series/parallel topology that stays within the AF M1D's verified load range;
4. confirm power, thermal, fuse and Tesla low-voltage integration.
