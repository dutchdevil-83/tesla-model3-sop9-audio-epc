## Installer architecture input

- **Audison AF M8.14 bit**: main DSP/amplifier for everything in front of the front seats / front stage.
- **Audison AF M4D**: everything behind the front seats, specifically rear woofers, rear midranges, and optional additional rear tweeters if desired.
- **Audison AF M1D**: intended/very likely dedicated amplifier for the retained Pioneer donor woofers. Exact loose-driver load and wiring remain an engineering verification gate.
- **Upper/headliner immersive speakers X569/X579**: omit for music. MB and a previous installer independently advised that these positions contribute little during normal OEM music playback, are used mainly for immersive/movie content, and tend to require strong attenuation in a music tune.
- **Dashboard center X595 is not the omitted speaker**. The Focal ICC T3Y 100 center remains in the actual quote.
